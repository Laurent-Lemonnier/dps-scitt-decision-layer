# One chain: notes for the pre-read (Case 18, `interop/one-chain/`)

1. One refund of 120.00 EUR runs through the four drafts in order: an agent asks to call `payments.refund.create`, and the Harness records a tool_call whose decision is `confirm`, then its `allow` outcome, in hash-chained Execution Receipts (Thaha).
2. Between the two, the finance controller approves the exact argument bytes the Harness hashed, in a Decision Provenance Standard record and action-binding attachment; after the run, the attachment's attempt and result cite the allow outcome and the tool_result by record-hash (Lemonnier).
3. An auditor writes a verdict record over the attachment at approval, the attachment after execution and the record's file, each by the SHA-256 of its exact bytes, rule `executed-as-approved:v1`; a checker written from Wright's text re-runs the rule and releases the record (Wright).
4. The merchant's dispute packet carries one Farley decision receipt whose `scope_conformance` is re-derived from the approved parameters and the executed action, agent anchors naming the approval event (the attachment's digest) and the verdict record's digest, a settlement anchor recording what moved, and the FRE 901 custody wrapper bound to a five-event ledger slice (Goshi over Farley).
5. What verifies: Thaha's verifier reaches CHECKS_PERFORMED over the Bundle (never PASS: eleven of his steps, 1, 4, 5, 7 and 11-17, are not performed by design); the Standard verifies eight dimensions, the issuer's seals and the person's signature; the checker releases the verdict with step 3's re-run equal to the stated result; the packet verifier reports VERIFIED with eight named checks.
6. Twelve joins across the four links hold on values already present in the records: one action digest end to end (confirm request_digest = approved parameters = packet `action_hash`), approval before allow in every record of it, the same record-hashes in the attachment, the ledger and the packet, settlement amount = result stage = approved amount, custody closing on the ledger, one person in every link, moments in order.
7. The same chain with 150.00 EUR executed for 120.00 approved: Thaha's confirm digest no longer matches the approval, the Standard's action_binding dimension fails, the verdict stating `not_as_approved` is released, the packet states `BREACH:amount_minor` with 150.00 moved and verifies as an honest packet about a breach; the twelve joins hold on it too.
8. Fifteen failing cases, each the valid chain changed in one place, are caught where expected: approval of other arguments (Thaha J1, the Standard, the verdict refused at step 3, the packet's SCOPE_HEADLINE_MISMATCH), verdict altered after the packet cited it (ANCHOR_UNRESOLVED), settlement 150 with headline 120, confirm without approval (only the Standard sees it), a packet citing a refused verdict (only the cross-link join sees it), custody or ledger event altered, settlement block absent or hash stale, receipt payload edited, wrong key, issuer_id not the kid, A2 altered after citation (verdict held), reason code missing, approval after the allow.
9. Numbers: 6 receipts, 2 attachments and 1 signature, 1 verdict record, 1 receipt + 5 ledger events + 3 custody events; 12 joins across links plus the 7 of case 17; 15/15 failing cases; two runs byte-identical.
10. Nothing in any of the four texts is changed to build this: the verdict cites the attachment's bytes as they are, the attachment cites the outcome receipt as any trace, Goshi's "approval event hash" takes a typed digest.
11. Limit one: everything is synthetic (test keys, fixed times, no real payment or person); the containers of the verdict and the Signed Statements are detached signatures and COSE_Sign1 without a Transparency Service receipt.
12. Limit two: links 1 and 3 reuse code the authors (Thaha, Wright) have been sent and are reviewing; link 4 is Laurent Lemonnier's composition from the Goshi and Farley texts alone, not reviewed by Goshi or Farley, with a sixteen-entry log of where the text left a construction open (`action_hash`, the approval event hash, the ledger event, the settlement headline, the failure names beyond the three the text gives).
13. What the authors' drafts would need for the joins to be checkable by their own verifiers rather than by this case: in Thaha's receipts an optional reference to the approval the confirm required (case 17, route 2); in Goshi's text a construction for `action_hash`, the approval event hash and the ledger event. Nothing in Wright's.
